MODEL APPLICATIONS

How to Apply

To be considered by Africa Model and Talent Scout (AMTS), please send your complete application by email to Scouting@africamts.com.

Please include all of the following information so our scouting team can review your application properly:

  1. Full name
  2. Age / date of birth
  3. Phone number and email address
  4. Current city and country
  5. Height and basic measurements — bust/chest, waist, hips and shoe size
  6. Recent photographs — clear headshot, full-length front, full-length side/profile and natural photos with minimal editing
  7. Videos — a short introduction video and a clear walking/runway video where applicable
  8. Instagram or social-media profile, if available
  9. Previous modeling/talent experience or portfolio link, if available. New faces are welcome to apply even without professional experience.

Email Subject

MODEL APPLICATION — FULL NAME — COUNTRY

Before You Send

Make sure your images and videos are clear and recent. If the files are too large for one email, include a shareable Google Drive, Dropbox or similar link and make sure viewing access is enabled.
