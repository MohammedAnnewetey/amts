Who we are

Scouting and developing Africa’s top models and creative talent.

Africa Model and Talent Scout (AMTS) is not an agency, but a company whose aim is to scout the top and best models and talents Africa has to offer, bring them together as a family, profile them, and provide them with jobs in their field. It is also a premiere scout and management company representing fashion talent around Africa.

Renowned for innovating model and talent management, AMTS has grown into a recognized and respected name in African modeling. The company has a diverse talent roster spanning women, men, hair and makeup artists, stylists and photographers. With an established foundation of talent and agents, AMTS continues to innovate and influence the industries of modeling, fashion and art.

Founded in 2013 in Ghana, Africa, under the leadership of CEO Mark Starr Kwakye and co-partner Derick Lawson, AMTS is making a name for itself in the African fashion and art market as well as abroad. The AMTS team is comprised of powerful, seasoned agents combined with a new generation of creative visionaries who will lead the company into the future.

Our Services

Management, visibility and opportunities.

Model Management

Through our well-maintained gallery of a diverse cross-section of models, we strive to meet client requirements for promotional activities including events, photo and product shoots, advertisements, runway shows and launches. Exclusive models also receive opportunities to take part in brand events and campaigns and grow as fashion icons.

Advertisement & Branding

We focus on brand image and publicity. Through our advertising and branding methodologies, we help create a distinct identity for our clients’ products and services, support penetration of target markets, enhance brand value and leave a lasting impact on audiences. From product launches to sponsored events, we provide a one-stop solution.

Talent Showcase

We maintain a diverse database of designer works and shoots, including fashion, jewelry and interiors. We provide opportunities to designers and photographers by involving their talent in our in-house publications and other promotional projects for clients’ products and services.

Portfolio & Opportunities

A portfolio is a model’s basic promotional tool, and a strong portfolio opens doors to recognition. We specialize in portfolios for both aspiring and established models and provide a platform to begin or develop a career in the glamour industry, with professionalism, variety and attention to detail.

New Faces & Profile Development

We promote new faces and help them create strong professional profiles. Our services include video profiles, portfolio shoots, features in selected magazines and guidance for developing a modeling career.
